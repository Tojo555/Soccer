Voice calls

Tap a marker on the pitch to call the next direction yourself. On a phone, tap the screen once so the voice can speak, and make sure silent mode is off.

Set it up

Find a solid wall and stand about 4 big steps from it. For the Wall drill, put markers (cones, shoes, water bottles) about 2 steps away from you: left, right, in front, and behind, plus the four diagonals for the 8-direction version.

For Pass and Dribble, tape an X on the ground (or use a cone) about 3½ big steps out in each of those directions.

Wall, two-touch

What to focus on

  • On your toes before the ball comes back, never flat-footed.
  • Know the call before the ball reaches you.
  • Inside of the foot, ankle locked, strike through the middle so it stays on the ground.
  • For the markers behind you, open your hips and receive on the half-turn.
  • On the dribble, small touches with the ball close. Get your head up between touches.
  • Switch feet every set. Try 60 seconds and count clean reps.
