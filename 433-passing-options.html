Try it yourself

Press and drag on the pitch to move the ball anywhere. The shape follows it live, the player nearest the ball shows his pass options, and, in the build-up versions, the crossing options appear when you drag the ball into a wide crossing spot. In the passing sequences the view zooms in on the box when the ball reaches the crossing area. Press Replay to go back to the passing sequence.

Rules used in every variation: the player on the ball always has a safe back pass (yellow) and an option to his left and right (white), either square or slightly forward or backward. The team slides toward the ball, and the holding midfielder on the far side stays just across the middle line, still central.
